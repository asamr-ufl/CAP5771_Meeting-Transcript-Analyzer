\f0\b\fs28 \cf2 Milestone 2 \'96 Reflection
\f1\b0 \

\f0\b Date: 20 Mar 2026
\f1\b0 \
\
This milestone was a big step forward in turning raw meeting transcripts into something meaningful and structured. At the beginning, the data felt messy and unorganised \'97 just blocks of text without clear boundaries between decisions, actions, and discussions. Over time, building a proper preprocessing pipeline made a huge difference. Cleaning, tokenising, and structuring the data gave me confidence that the models were learning from something reliable rather than noise.\
\
One of the most interesting parts was seeing how different models behaved. I initially expected more complex models to perform significantly better, but surprisingly, simpler models like Random Forest performed consistently well. This made me realise that good feature engineering and clean data often matter more than just increasing model complexity.\
\
Another key learning was understanding where the model struggles. The confusion between \'93decision\'94 and \'93action\'94 labels showed that even humans might find it tricky to clearly distinguish between them in some cases. This gave us a better appreciation of real-world ambiguity in NLP tasks.\
\
Overall, this milestone helped us connect theory with practice \'97 especially around preprocessing, feature extraction, and model evaluation. It also highlighted the importance of iterative improvement rather than trying to get everything perfect in one attempt.\
